Dynamis App

Privacy Policy

Your reading data stays with you. This policy explains what the app stores, what it does not collect, and how optional backups work.

Local Data Only

Dynamis stores reading progress, preferences, and related settings locally on your device. This information never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable cloud backup.

On-device storage User-controlled No hidden sync

Google Drive Backups

The app offers an optional backup and restore feature using Google Drive. When you turn this on, your backup data is stored in your own Google Drive account.

The developer does not collect, access, store, transmit, or share the contents of these backups. Google Drive access is used solely to create or restore backups at your request.

You stay in control. Backup access is only used when you choose to back up or restore.

No Ads, No Tracking

Dynamis does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services. The app is built to focus on your reading, not your data.
