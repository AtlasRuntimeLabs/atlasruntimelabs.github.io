Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the Dynamis application (“the App”) handles your information, including the data stored on your device, the information the App does not collect, and the operation of the optional backup feature.

On-Device Data Storage

Dynamis stores your reading progress, preferences, and related settings locally on your device. This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to the developer or any third party unless you explicitly enable cloud backup.

On-device storage User-controlled No background sync

No Advertising or Tracking

Dynamis does not incorporate advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies of any kind. The application is designed to operate without collecting information about your usage or behavior.

Optional Google Drive Backups

Dynamis offers an optional backup and restore feature powered by Google Drive. The feature is disabled by default and, when enabled, operates entirely within your own Google Drive account.

Storage location
Your personal Google Drive account
Developer access
None — backup contents are never read, stored, or shared
Scope of access
Limited to creating and restoring the backups you request
Default state
Disabled until you choose to turn it on

You may disable backups or remove stored backup data at any time from within the App.
